## Overview

**Speak Savvy** is an AI-powered public speaking and speech coaching application that provides personalised feedback to help users become more effective communicators.

Users can submit a speech, presentation, or lecture, which Speak Savvy analyses across multiple aspects of delivery. Instead of simply identifying problems, the platform explains **why** something could be improved and provides actionable suggestions for **how to improve it**.

Speak Savvy can be used by students, lecturers, professionals, and anyone looking to improve their public speaking and presentation skills.

---

## Inspiration

The idea for Speak Savvy came from observing how difficult it can be for people to receive meaningful feedback on their public speaking.

People regularly practise speeches and presentations, but feedback is often limited to general comments such as "speak more clearly" or "be more engaging." These comments do not explain what went wrong or how the speaker can improve.

This problem is particularly noticeable in university education. Lecture attendance can decline significantly throughout a semester, highlighting the importance of engaging and effective communication.

Speak Savvy was created to make detailed speaking feedback more accessible by using AI to analyse speech delivery and provide personalised explanations and recommendations.

---

## What It Does

Speak Savvy analyses speech and presentation delivery across areas such as:

- **Pace** — identifies whether the speaker is speaking too quickly or slowly
- **Pauses** — evaluates the use of pauses and whether they improve emphasis
- **Clarity** — identifies areas where delivery could be clearer
- **Filler words** — detects repeated words such as "um", "uh" and "like"
- **Engagement** — evaluates aspects of delivery that contribute to audience engagement
- **Structure** — provides feedback on how effectively ideas are communicated
- **Confidence** — identifies delivery patterns that may affect how confident the speaker sounds

Rather than only giving a score, Speak Savvy provides **personalised explanations** of the issues it identifies and practical recommendations for improving future speeches.

Users can also track their feedback over time to see whether their speaking performance is improving.

---

## How It Works

The application follows a simple workflow:

    Speech / Presentation
            ↓
       Upload / Submit
            ↓
       Speech Analysis
            ↓
        AI Evaluation
            ↓
    Personalised Feedback
            ↓
      Recommendations
            ↓
    Track Progress Over Time

The system is designed to answer three key questions:

1. **What did I do?**
2. **Why could it be improved?**
3. **How can I improve it?**

This turns speech analysis into an actionable coaching experience rather than simply providing a performance score.

## Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was developing AI analysis that could provide **meaningful and actionable feedback** rather than generic advice.

Speech quality is subjective and depends on factors such as context, audience, purpose, and speaking style. Designing feedback that remains useful across different types of speakers therefore required careful consideration of the evaluation criteria.

Other challenges included:

- Processing and analysing speech content
- Designing useful feedback criteria
- Presenting complex AI analysis in a simple interface
- Maintaining privacy when handling speech and presentation data
- Making recommendations specific enough to be actionable

## Accomplishments

We successfully developed an AI-driven platform capable of turning speech analysis into personalised coaching feedback.

Rather than simply telling users whether their speech was good or bad, Speak Savvy focuses on explaining **specific areas for improvement and how to address them**.

The platform demonstrates how AI can be used as an accessible personal speaking coach for students, educators, and professionals.
